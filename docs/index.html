Guangting Zheng, Yiyuan Zhang, Tao Yang, Yunpeng Chen, Rui Zhu et al. ▲ 0 by @Yiyuan
Latent generative models typically follow a two-stage pipeline, training a variational autoencoder for reconstruction and then a generative model on the frozen latent space. Since reconstruction-optimized latents are not necessarily generation-friendly, jointly training both models is an appealing alternative.
